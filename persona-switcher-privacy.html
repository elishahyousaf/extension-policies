Privacy Policy

Smart Persona Switcher for Marketers (PersonaFlow)

Last updated: October 12, 2026

Overview

Smart Persona Switcher for Marketers ("PersonaFlow", "the extension") helps marketers switch between up to 5 brand or work personas, each with its own set of websites, colors, notes, and reminders. This policy explains what data the extension handles and how it is protected.

Data the extension stores

All of the following is created by you and stored only on your own device, in your browser's local extension storage (chrome.storage). It is never transmitted to us or to any third party:

  • Persona definitions — persona names, custom colors, avatars, and the website URLs linked to each persona.
  • Persona notes — private notes you attach to each persona.
  • Reminder settings — the reminders you configure per persona (schedules managed by the extension's built-in alarm system).
  • Usage statistics — counts shown in the in-extension usage dashboard, such as tab launches and persona activations.

How the extension uses browser permissions

  • Tabs — to open, group, and activate browser tabs when you switch personas. The extension does not read page content.
  • Scripting / activeTab — to display a small color-coded banner on supported pages showing which persona is active. No page data is read, collected, or modified beyond displaying this indicator, and it only runs when you activate a persona.
  • Storage — to save the persona data listed above, locally on your device.
  • Alarms & notifications — to deliver the persona reminders you configure. Nothing else.
  • Host access — limited to specific well-known websites where the persona indicator banner is displayed.

What we do NOT do

  • No tracking. We do not track you across websites.
  • No external servers. The extension works 100% locally; it makes no network requests and sends no data anywhere.
  • No data sale or sharing. Your data is never sold, rented, or shared with any third party, for any purpose.
  • No remote code. All code is bundled with the extension; nothing is loaded from the internet.

Data retention and deletion

Your persona data remains in your browser until you delete it. You can remove individual personas (and their notes, reminders, and stats) inside the extension at any time. Uninstalling the extension removes all data it stored.

Children

The extension is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect any data from children.

Changes to this policy
