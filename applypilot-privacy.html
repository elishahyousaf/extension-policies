ApplyPilot — Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 12, 2026

Data Collection

ApplyPilot stores your job-seeker profile (name, email, phone, location, LinkedIn URL, experience, skills, education, summary) locally in your browser using chrome.storage.local. This data never leaves your device.

Data Use

Your profile is used solely to: (1) auto-fill job application forms on pages you choose, (2) generate cover letters from job descriptions you paste, (3) compute ATS match scores. No data is transmitted to any server.

Data Sharing

We do not sell, share, or transmit your data to third parties. There are no analytics, no tracking, and no external network requests.

Permissions

  • storage — save your profile locally.
  • activeTab + scripting — fill forms only on the tab you explicitly activate via the popup button.
