CartHawk Privacy Policy

CartHawk Privacy Policy (v1.0.0)

Plain-English privacy policy for the CartHawk Chrome extension. Last updated: 2026-10-12.

The short version

CartHawk stores everything on your device and sends nothing anywhere. There is no account, no sign-up, no analytics, and no server.

What CartHawk stores (on your device only)

When you track a product, CartHawk saves in your browser's local storage:

  • the product's page address (URL), title, and retailer name
  • the prices it observes and when it saw them
  • the target price you set
  • your settings (check frequency, notification toggles, currency display)

That's it. This data never leaves your computer.

What CartHawk does with web pages

  • On product pages of Amazon, eBay, Walmart, Target, and Best Buy, CartHawk reads the visible product title and price so it can show the "Track this price" badge and record price history for items you track.
  • On checkout pages with a coupon/promo field, it shows a small disclosure notice. That's all it does there.
  • CartHawk does not read your email, your other tabs, your passwords, or any site outside the listed retailers.
  • CartHawk does not modify, replace, or inject links on any page.

Network use

  • CartHawk works offline-first. It makes no network requests of its own except one optional case: if you turn on the experimental background re-check in Settings, the extension will try to fetch your tracked product pages directly. This is off by default.
  • CartHawk loads no remote code, fonts, images, or libraries. Everything is bundled inside the extension package.

Affiliate disclosure

v1 of CartHawk earns no commissions and injects no affiliate links. If a future version ever earns a commission through a tracked link, it will say so clearly in the product before you buy — the same disclosure you see in the extension today.

Your control

  • Export: Settings → "Export price history (CSV)" downloads everything.
  • Delete: Settings → "Delete all tracked products and history" wipes it instantly. Uninstalling the extension also removes all stored data.
  • Notifications: fully toggleable in Settings.

Changes to this policy

If this policy ever changes, the new version ships inside the extension update and the "last updated" date above changes with it. We will never quietly expand data collection.

Contact

Questions about privacy: open the extension's Settings page — support is handled through the Chrome Web Store listing.

This policy applies to the CartHawk Chrome extension. For questions, contact the developer through the Chrome Web Store listing.
