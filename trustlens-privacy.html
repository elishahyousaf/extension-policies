TrustLens Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 12, 2026

TrustLens ("the extension") is built on a simple principle: a privacy tool must not collect data. This policy is short because there is almost nothing to disclose.

What TrustLens collects

Nothing. TrustLens:

  • makes zero network requests — it works fully offline;
  • has no accounts, no sign-in, and no analytics;
  • sends no data to the developer or any third party, ever.

What TrustLens stores (on your device only)

Using your browser's local storage, TrustLens keeps:

  1. Your settings — the risk-alert threshold and whether monitoring is on.
  2. An extension snapshot — the names, versions, permissions, and enabled states of your installed extensions, used only to detect changes (new installs, updates that add permissions).
  3. An event log — the 50 most recent security events (installs, enables, disables, uninstalls, permission changes), viewable in the popup's Activity tab and deletable anytime from Settings.

All of this stays on your device. Uninstalling TrustLens deletes it.

Permissions and why

| Permission | Why it's needed | |---|---| | management | List your installed extensions, read their declared permissions, and let you disable/uninstall risky ones. This is the extension's core function. | | storage | Keep the settings, snapshot, and event log above — locally only. | | alarms | Run the once-daily background audit. |

TrustLens deliberately requests no host permissions — it cannot see, read, or modify any website you visit.

Changes to this policy

If this policy ever changes, the new version will ship with the extension update and the "last updated" date above will change. The no-data-collection principle will not change without a major version bump and a clear notice in the store listing.

Contact

Questions about this policy: open an issue on the project's repository or contact the developer through the Chrome Web Store listing page.
