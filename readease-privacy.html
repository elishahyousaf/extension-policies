ReadEase Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 12, 2026

ReadEase is designed so that your reading stays yours. This policy explains, in plain English, what happens with your information.

What data is collected

None. ReadEase does not collect, store, transmit, or sell any personal data.

Settings

Your settings — font choices, spacing sliders, simplification level, presets, voice and rate preferences — are saved with chrome.storage, which keeps them on your device only, inside your own browser. Nothing is uploaded anywhere.

Network

ReadEase makes no network requests, ever. There are no analytics, no telemetry, no crash reporting, no remote servers, and no third-party services involved.

Accounts

There are no accounts. Nothing to sign up for, nothing to log in to.

Read-aloud

The read-aloud feature uses your device's built-in speech engine (the same voices your operating system provides). The text being read never leaves your device — it is not sent to any server for processing.

Children's use

Because no data is collected at all, there is nothing to share about anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy ever changes, the updated version will ship with the extension and the "Last updated" date above will change.
